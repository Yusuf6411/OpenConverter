# Theora

Decoder in `crates/oc-theora`, written from the Theora specification (`docs/specs/theora.pdf`); reading of Ogg video in `crates/oc-video/src/ogv.rs`. Decoding only: there is no Theora encoder, so Ogg video is a read-only input format.

**Decoder.** All three header packets (identification, comment, setup) and every frame type: intra and inter frames, all eight macroblock modes (golden frame references and four-vector macroblocks included), both motion-vector codes, block-level quantiser indices (one to three per frame), 4:2:0, 4:2:2 and 4:4:4, odd picture sizes and offsets, DC prediction, the exact integer inverse DCT (and its DC-only shortcut), the loop filter, and empty packets (repeat the last frame). The specification's own tables (Hilbert order, zig-zag, DC predictor weights, mode alphabets, run-length codes) are in the source; the quantiser matrices and Huffman trees come from the stream's setup header, so no table was copied from another implementation.

**How it was checked.** `tools/make_theora_fixtures.py` encodes nine clips with libtheora (through ffmpeg: 4:2:0, 4:2:2, 4:4:4, odd size, quality 1 to 10, bit-rate mode, motion from the mandelbrot, life and test-card sources) and records ffmpeg's per-frame MD5 of the decoded planes. `crates/oc-theora/tests/ffmpeg.rs` requires every frame to match: 154 frames, all modes, one to three quantiser indices per frame. `tests/robust.rs` feeds damaged headers and frames (flipped bytes, truncation) and requires errors, never panics. Fuzz target `theora`. Speed: about 190 frames per second at 1280x720 (single thread, release build).

**Ogg video.** `oc_video::ogv` reads Theora and Vorbis streams (in any order, several of each) into the packet model: Theora times follow from the frame rate, Vorbis times from the block sizes in the setup header. Packets are kept as they are, so Ogg to Matroska copies them without decoding (Matroska's `V_THEORA` keeps the three headers Xiph-laced in `CodecPrivate`, as the Ogg mapping does). Converting to MP4, WebM or GIF decodes the pictures; 4:2:2 and 4:4:4 chroma is averaged down to 4:2:0 once decoded, as for every other video here.

**Not done.** Theora encoding; the VP3 header defaults (streams without a setup header); Ogg streams other than Theora and Vorbis (Opus, Speex, FLAC, Skeleton and KATE are skipped); chained Ogg files are read as one set of streams; the odd chroma offset of picture regions whose x or y offset is odd is approximated (chroma is cropped from the floor of half the offset).
